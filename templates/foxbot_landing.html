CREATOR CONNECTION

Connect FoxBot in four clear steps.

Start in the FoxBot profile chat, activate your access, then test FoxBot inside your own Blaze channel.

Important: a Connected Creators badge does not mean FoxBot is listening yet.

The badge only confirms that your profile was registered. Commands work after your access is active, your Blaze channel is resolved, and the live listener is monitoring it.

01 FOXBOT PROFILE CHAT

Open the FoxBot Blaze profile

Visit blaze.stream/foxbotai. Do not start by typing the activation command in your own channel.

02 START YOUR TRIAL

Type !join

Send !join in the FoxBot profile chat. This starts your seven-day trial and registers your creator access.

03 CONFIRM ACCESS

Type !access

Confirm that FoxBot reports an active trial or subscription. Allow 15–30 seconds for your Blaze channel to enter the listener targets.

04 TEST YOUR CHANNEL

Type !foxhelp in your chat

Return to your own Blaze channel and send !foxhelp. A FoxBot reply confirms that your channel is actively monitored.

No reply yet?

Run !access again in the FoxBot profile chat, confirm your exact Blaze handle, and check that the live listener is running.
